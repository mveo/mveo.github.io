Author Guidelines and Submissions

The MVEO Workshop will be held in person from 23–26 November 2026 in Lancaster, UK. If you are unable to attend, you may nominate one of your co-authors (or another representative) to present the paper on your behalf. However, if neither you nor a representative is able to present the work, the paper will be withdrawn and will not be included in the workshop proceedings.

1. Prepare the anonymous, 14-page (references excluded) submission using the BMVC template and following the paper guidelines.

2. Submit at TBA.
